§4 · Money: sources → demands
Rupees freed by bid cuts, plus a growth pool of unspent plan money (priced at the ROAS floor, open only while the portfolio runs ≥ 5% above it), fund bid raises and budget-bound campaigns. A rupee moves only when the receiver's marginal ROAS ≥ 1.2× the source's and P(better) > 0.8. All moves are solved together as one transport LP. Freed rupees that nobody qualifies for are released.

§6 · Base data
Sources. Simulated by gpc/market.py (hidden from policies). Ad-slot positions, slot view / conversion / price ratios, daypart search shares and direct-ROAS ranges by keyword type are calibrated to aggregated production Blinkit data (industry rank curve, rank × hour bid curve, MMM cell outputs); see CALIBRATION.md. Traversal: gpc/engine/. Guardrails: gpc/guardrails.py.
Caveats. All brands, products, campaigns and numbers are synthetic. Grid values are the engine's estimates from observed data, not the market's truth; the gap between the two is part of the challenge.
